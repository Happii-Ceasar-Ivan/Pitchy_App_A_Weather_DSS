# Design System: Pitchy

<!-- impeccable:design-map -->
Name: Pitchy
Colors: primary: shell-navy #031A31 (Midnight Navy); secondary: line-open-meteo #13786A; neutral: on-shell #FFFFFF; accent: accent-glacier-deep #1D5A86 (on the shell: Glacier #8BB9D9); fixed: state-current-fg #0B5C43; custom-swatch: swatch-navy #0F2540 (+77 more in the sidecar)
Fonts: Switzer (display, headline, headline-small, title, title-medium, title-small, body, body-large, body-small, label, label-medium, label-small); IBMPlexMono (figure-mono, Line Board figures only)
Spacing: 4/8 rhythm, PitchySpace in lib/core/theme/spacing.dart: xxs 2px (micro gaps only), xs 4px, sm 8px, md 12px, lg 16px, xl 24px, xxl 32px, xxxl 48px, touch-target 48px
Radius: scrollbar 4px, sm 8px, card 12px, lg 16px, pill 999px
North Star: "The Line Board"
Rules:
- The Two Layers Rule. Themes change only shell, ground, surface, hairline, ink and accent.
- The Custom Two Rule. Custom changes only the shell and the accent, and both come from the eight fixed swatches.
- The Cool Lines Rule. Source line colours are cool hues only (teal through blue and violet to plum); never yellow, orange or red.
- The Icon and Word Rule. No state is shown by colour alone.
- The Hazard Severity Rule. One icon per level (advisory info_outline, warning warning_amber_rounded, danger warning); hazard colours are fixed, never palette.accent (P-18, P-19).
- The Tabular Everywhere Rule. Every text style carries tabular figures; Switzer's default figures are tabular and the font has no tnum feature.
- The PHT Label Rule. Every displayed time is Philippine Time with "PHT" shown, whatever the device's time zone.
- The Font Privacy Rule. All fonts are bundled; nothing loads a font at runtime (P-28).
- The Flat Board Rule. No shadows on the board; one navy-tinted shadow for overlays only.
- The One Hairline Rule. Every rule, border and divider is 1px in the hairline colour.
- The Rare Motion Rule. Animate only overlays, expand and collapse, a new chat reply, the Updating spinner, press feedback, step changes, validation messages, the trend chart's Value switch (the drawing only; readout and range text change at once) and the trend hour marker; never tabs, theme switches, values, or safety and state elements.
- The Reduced Motion Rule. Opacity only, 150ms or less; size changes are instant; exits faster than entrances.
- The Plain Curve Rule. No ease-in, bounce or stagger. Scale only as the 0.97 press on standalone buttons; one critically damped spring only for the trend hour marker (P-29).
Don't:
- Don't theme, tint or let users customise the state colours or the safety ramp.
- Don't use palette.accent or any themed colour for a hazard level.
- Don't use yellow, orange or red as a source line colour, a swatch or decoration.
- Don't show any state by colour alone.
- Don't add shadows or elevation to cards, bars, chips or the rail; only overlays get the one navy-tinted shadow.
- Don't animate tabs, theme switches, values, safety or state elements; don't use ease-in, bounce or stagger; don't scale anything but a pressed standalone button (never cards, rows, data chips, nav items or hazard elements).
- Don't show stale, missing or unavailable data in a way that could read as Current, and don't hide it.
- Don't set figures without tabular spacing.
- Don't load any font at runtime or add a font-fetching package.
- Don't bring the landing page's Racing Red, or any landing-page style, into the app.
- Don't treat the placeholder logo as final artwork.
Do:
- Do take every colour from the palette extensions (PitchyPalette, StateColors, SafetyColors), never from literal colours in widgets.
- Do show every data state as a pill with its icon and word, and keep Stale data on screen with its dashed stamp.
- Do give each new weather source one fixed, cool line colour (light and dark variants) and use it only for that source's dot, rail stop and validity bar.
- Do label every time PHT, and name the day when it isn't today in PHT.
- Do colour an official alert only from PAGASA's own level (P-18): watch, yellow rainfall and signal 1 are advisory; advisory, orange rainfall and signals 2-3 are warning; warning, red rainfall and signals 4-5 are danger; anything else stays neutral. Always show PAGASA's words with the colour.
- Do use one icon per hazard level and colour it from SafetyColors.
- Do keep tap targets at least 48px, and keep cards flat with a 1px hairline border and a 12px radius.
- Do set Line Board figures (rail times, kickoff time, trend axis and readout) in IBM Plex Mono digits and everything else in Switzer.
- Do keep the Pitchy logo left of the screen name in every top bar.
- Do make reduced motion opacity-only and no longer than 150ms, and every exit faster than its entrance.
