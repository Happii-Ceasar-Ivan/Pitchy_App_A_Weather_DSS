# Design System: Pitchy

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Line Board"**

Pitchy reads like a Metro Manila train line board. Each weather source is a line with its own fixed colour; its state is the stop, and its "current until" time reads like the next departure. A Midnight Navy shell (top bar, navigation rail, kickoff strip) frames a near-white work ground where flat, hairline-bordered cards carry the data. The board is built to be scanned at a glance, one-handed, in sun and glare: big tabular figures, an icon and a word on every state, and nothing that fades away when it gets old. Stale data stays on the board and gets a dashed stamp.

The components are standard Material 3 on purpose. The world comes in through the palette, the Switzer type, the density and one signature component, the source rail. Android and Windows get the same look because every font is bundled with the app and there are no per-platform styles. Colour is split into two layers that never mix. The themed layer (shell, ground, ink, accent) changes with Light, Dark, Pitch Green or Custom. The fixed layer (data-state colours and the safety ramp) means the same thing in every theme. The board refinement kept this world and changed its material: Midnight Navy and Glacier in place of the old navy and signal blue, Switzer in place of Roboto, IBM Plex Mono for Line Board figures only, one overlay shadow, and one motion language.

The system rejects the usual weather-app look of a hero temperature over a grid of identical tonal cards. Values sit in a small 2x2 grid (4 columns on wide windows) under the source rail. Each value is one tap from its proof: the source, fetch and validity times, and the spread across sources.

Brand and product are separate. The landing page has its own brand colour (Racing Red); it never enters the app. Nothing in the app takes a colour, type style or motion from the landing page.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Midnight Navy shell around a cool near-white ground. The board is flat; only overlays cast one navy-tinted shadow.
- One fixed, cool line colour per weather source, used for its rail dot, validity bar and the dot on its value card.
- Every data state is a pill with an icon and an uppercase word. Stale also gets a dashed outline.
- Switzer for all UI (default figures are tabular); IBM Plex Mono only for Line Board figures.
- Fixed safety and state colours sit outside every theme; one icon per hazard level.
- Motion is rare and short: overlays, expand and collapse, the new chat reply, the Updating spinner and press feedback.
- All times are labelled PHT.

## Colors

A cool, Midnight Navy-anchored themed layer sits above a fixed layer of state and safety colours that no theme can reach.

### Primary
- **Midnight Navy** (shell-navy): the top bar, the navigation rail on wide windows and the kickoff strip. In Light it is also the ink colour, so the shell and the text share one hue. Raised tone for the selected rail item: shell-navy-raised.
- **Glacier** (on-shell-accent): selection and focus on the shell only (rail indicator icon and label, focus ring on navy). It is 2.09:1 on white, so it is never used on light surfaces.
- **Glacier Deep** (accent-glacier-deep): primary actions, text buttons, links, the cursor, selection and the 2px focus ring on light surfaces (7.35:1 on white). It is a darker tone of the Glacier hue. The nav-bar and chip selected tint is the accent at 14% over the surface. Hover is 4% of the content colour, press 8%, keyboard focus 12% accent.

### Secondary
- **Source line colours** (line-open-meteo teal, line-pagasa-atmo violet, line-pagasa-ecmwf blue, line-dost-asti slate, with lighter variants for Dark): fixed per source id and used only to identify the source. Unknown sources fall back to ink-muted.

### Neutral
- **Work Ground** (ground): the scaffold behind cards.
- **Card White** (surface): cards, the source rail, dialogs, the drawer and the bottom navigation bar.
- **Hairline Grey** (hairline): every border, divider, the rail track and the validity track.
- **Midnight Ink** (ink) and **Muted Slate Ink** (ink-muted): primary text, and secondary text such as labels, captions, body-small and unselected navigation.

### Themes
- **Light**: the base values above.
- **Dark**: dark-* tokens, retuned. The shell is near-black navy (dark-shell), ground and surface step up from it, and the accent is Glacier (dark-accent) with Midnight Navy text on it. On-shell-accent is Glacier here too.
- **Pitch Green**: pitch-* tokens. A deep green shell, green-tinted ground and ink, and a green accent. On-shell-accent is plain white.
- **Custom**: the user picks a shell and an accent from the eight swatch-* colours (unchanged). Shell-raised is white at 8% over the shell, on-shell-muted is white at 78% over the shell, and on-shell-accent is white. Every other role keeps its Light value.

### Fixed: data states
- Five state pairs (Current, Stale, Updating, Missing, Unavailable), each a foreground on a tinted background with at least 4.5:1 contrast. They have a light and a dark variant for contrast only; themes never change them. Dark Stale foreground is #E3BF95.

### Fixed: safety ramp
- **Advisory yellow** (safety-advisory), **Warning orange** (safety-warning), **Danger red** (safety-danger), each with its own on-colour. These are the official-alert colours (P-18).
- **Calm info** (calm-info, calm-info-border, on-calm-info; dark variants too): the safety-level "advisory" pill (P-19), a calm blue-grey that is not amber, for example "below the cooling-break level". It is fixed like the rest of the ramp.
- **Request Error** (request-error) is a failed request, not a hazard, and is also the Material `error` role. In Dark, error text and icons use the lighter #FF8A80 (request-error-ink-dark) because #9F1D1D is 2.03:1 on the dark surface. The backend or the issuing agency decides which level applies; the app never does.

### Named Rules
**The Two Layers Rule.** Themes change only shell, ground, surface, hairline, ink and accent. Data-state colours and the safety ramp are fixed in every theme and never take a custom colour.

**The Custom Two Rule.** Custom changes only the shell and the accent, and both come from the eight fixed swatches. Every swatch passes 4.5:1 against white, and none is red, orange or yellow, so a themed colour can never pass for a safety colour.

**The Cool Lines Rule.** Source line colours are cool hues only. The safety ramp (yellow, orange, red) is never used as a line colour or as decoration.

**The Icon and Word Rule.** No state is shown by colour alone. Every state shows an icon and a word, and Stale also gets a dashed outline, so it still reads in greyscale.

**The Hazard Severity Rule.** A hazard level has one icon everywhere: advisory info_outline, warning warning_amber_rounded, danger warning. Hazard colours come from SafetyColors, are fixed, and are never palette.accent or any themed colour. The backend's label is always shown with the colour. Official alerts map from PAGASA's own level (P-18); safety levels map from the backend's severity (P-19).

## Typography

**Display Font:** Switzer (bundled static OTFs at 400, 500, 600 and 700)
**Body Font:** Switzer
**Label/Mono Font:** Switzer for labels; IBM Plex Mono (400, 500, 600) for Line Board figures only

**Character:** One grotesque face for the whole interface, weighted for glare. Values and large headings are bold (700) and tighten slightly (-0.5px to -0.3px) so they look firm; titles and labels are semibold (600); body text is regular. Light-on-dark text gets a touch more tracking (body +0.1px, labels +0.2px) with the same line box. Sizes and line heights are set explicitly so skeletons never depend on font metrics. Switzer is licensed under the ITF Free Font License, is fetched at build time by tool/fetch_switzer.dart and is git-ignored; a missing file fails the build.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, 36px, 44px line, -0.5px): reserved. It is set in the theme but not used by any built surface yet.
- **Headline** (700, 28px, 36px line, -0.4px): the large value on a weather card. A card with no value drops to Title.
- **Headline small** (700, 24px, 32px line, -0.3px): set in the theme, kept for screen-level headings.
- **Title** (600, 22px, 28px line): the top-bar screen name, on the shell colour.
- **Title medium** (600, 16px, 24px line): the unit after a value, and message-state titles.
- **Title small** (600, 14px, 20px line): source names on the rail and the safety-level pill (set at 700 there).
- **Body** (400, 14px, 20px line): detail rows and banner messages. Banners use 700.
- **Body small** (400, 12px, 16px line, ink-muted by default): "Current until ..." lines, slot times and detail labels.
- **Label** (600, 14px, 20px line, 0.1px): card quantity labels and the rail header, in ink-muted, plus button text.
- **Label medium / small** (600, 12px / 11px, 16px line): navigation labels (unselected 500), validity-bar ticks, and state-pill words (uppercase, 700) in regular and dense pills.
- **Figure mono** (IBM Plex Mono, weight capped at 600, tracking 0): only the digits of the source rail's time figures, the kickoff time and the trend chart's time axis and readout. The colon, en dash, spaces, AM/PM and PHT stay in Switzer (PitchyFigures.figureSpans). Everywhere else times stay Switzer.

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Everywhere Rule.** Every text style carries tabular figures. Switzer's default figures are already tabular and the font has no tnum feature; the theme still requests tabular figures on every role as a fallback. Numbers never set in proportional figures.

**The PHT Label Rule.** Every displayed time is Philippine Time with "PHT" shown, whatever the device's time zone. A fetch time from another PHT day names the day. The label is only dropped next to a tick whose window end already shows PHT.

**The Font Privacy Rule.** Every font is bundled with the app. Nothing loads a font at runtime, and no font package that fetches from a network is a dependency (test/app/font_privacy_test.dart guards it).

## Layout

Phone first, with one breakpoint for navigation and one for the card grid.

- **Navigation:** below 600px wide, a bottom navigation bar (Dashboard, PitChyAT, Settings). At 600px and wider, an 88px navigation rail on the shell colour, with a hairline vertical divider between it and the content.
- **Gutter and measure:** content keeps a 16px side gutter. On wide windows it is centred at a 960px maximum width.
- **Card grid:** 2 columns below 720px of content width and 4 columns at 720px and above, with a 10px gap. At most 4 cards show at first, with a "Show N more" text button below. Only one card is expanded at a time; on 2-column layouts the expanded card spans the full row.
- **Large text:** from a text scale of 1.5, layouts switch to one card per row and stacked headers so state words and values are never cut.
- **Rhythm:** 4/8px steps with a few set values: 14px card insets, 10px rail-row padding, 16px gutters and 24px message-state padding. Expanded detail rows put the label and value side by side (72px label column) and stack them below 280px of card width.
- **Targets:** every button has a minimum height of 48px.

## Elevation & Depth

The board is flat. Cards, the app bar (including when scrolled under), the navigation bar, chips and the rail all have elevation 0 and no surface tint. Depth comes from tone: a white surface on a cool ground, a navy shell around the content, and the raised-shell tone for the selected rail item. Only things that float over the board cast a shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Overlay** (elevation 6; shadow colour #031A31 at 12% in light themes, black at 40% in Dark): the drawer, dialogs and snackbars.
- **Menu** (elevation 3, same shadow colour, plus a 1px hairline border): popup menus and menus.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Board Rule.** No shadows on the board. Surfaces are separated by tone and one hairline border. The one navy-tinted shadow (AppTheme.overlayShadow) is for overlays only: drawer, dialogs, snackbars, menus.

## Shapes

Three working radii plus the pill: 8px (inputs, chips, menus, tooltips), 12px (cards and the safety-level pill), 16px (dialogs, snackbars and the drawer's open edge), fully rounded pills (999px) for state pills, and 4px on scrollbar thumbs. Borders and dividers are a single 1px hairline. The source rail's marks are drawn heavier than the hairline because they are graphics, not rules: a 2px rail line, 8px-radius stop dots with a 3px ring (filled when Current, hollow otherwise), a 4px validity track with a 6px bar in the line colour, and a 2px kickoff tick in ink. Keyboard focus is a 2px accent ring, never only a colour wash.

### Named Rules
**The One Hairline Rule.** Every rule, border and divider is 1px in the hairline colour. The only other outlines are the Stale stamp (a 1.5px dash, 4px on and 3px off, in the Stale foreground colour) and the 2px focus ring.

## Motion

Motion lives with the components it touches; the tokens are PitchyMotion in lib/core/theme/motion.dart.

- **Durations:** press 100ms, quick 150ms, standard 200ms (route entrance, new chat reply), expand 220ms, overlay 250ms, exit 150ms.
- **Curves:** enter cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1) for anything arriving; move cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1) for something travelling between two places; plain ease for colour changes.
- **Pushed pages** (Settings, About, Match setup): fade with a 2% rise, 200ms in and 150ms out. The three main tabs sit in an IndexedStack and never transition.

### Named Rules
**The Rare Motion Rule.** Animate only overlays opening and closing, expand and collapse, a newly arrived chat reply, the Updating spinner and press feedback. Never tab switches, values updating, or safety and data-state elements (safety banners, alerts, state pills and stale stamps never animate in).

**The Reduced Motion Rule.** When the system asks for fewer animations, movement goes and only short opacity changes stay, at 150ms or less. Expand, collapse and scrolling become instant, the rise becomes 0 and page transitions are opacity only. Exits are always faster than entrances (collapse 150ms against expand 220ms).

**The Plain Curve Rule.** No ease-in, no bounce, no stagger and no scale on entrances. Entrances use the strong ease-out.

## Components

Standard Material, kept flat and dense. The board's character comes from the source rail and the state pills.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** Material 3 stadium defaults; no custom radius.
- **Filled:** accent background with on-accent text, at least 48px tall, elevation 0. Disabled is ink at 12% with ink at 38% text.
- **Outlined:** accent text with a 1px hairline border, at least 48px tall. "Try again" in error states is an outlined button with an icon.
- **Text:** accent text, at least 48 x 48px. Used for "Show N more" / "Show fewer".
- **Focus:** a 2px ring (accent on outlined and text buttons, ink on filled) plus a 12% wash. Press 8%, hover 4%.

### Chips (state pills)
- **Style:** a fully rounded pill on the state background, with the state icon and the uppercase state word in 700 on the state foreground. Regular: 16px icon, 12px label, 4px x 10px padding. Dense: 14px icon, 11px label, 2px x 6px padding.
- **State:** Current (check_circle), Stale (history, dashed stamp), Updating (sync), Missing (help_outline), Unavailable (cloud_off). Screen readers hear "Data state: <word>".

### Safety level pill
A 12px-radius pill at least 40px tall with a 20px icon and the backend's label verbatim (Title small at 700). Advisory: calm-info fill with a calm-info-border outline and the info_outline icon. Warning: safety-warning fill, warning_amber_rounded. Danger: safety-danger fill, warning. No known level: ground fill with a hairline border and an info icon. Official alert panels use the same icons with PAGASA's yellow, orange and red (P-18).

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 12px.
- **Background:** surface.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** a 1px hairline. A Stale weather card puts the dashed Stale stamp on the whole card.
- **Internal Padding:** 14px for weather cards; 16px sides with 14px top and 8px bottom for the source rail.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** outlined, unfilled, 8px radius. Resting border is ink-muted at 60%; floating labels are body small.
- **Focus:** a 2px accent border. **Error:** request-error ink border, with the message in body small.

### Overlays
- **Dialog:** surface, 16px radius, elevation 6. **Drawer:** surface, 16px radius on the open edge only, elevation 6. **Snackbar:** inverse surface, floating, 16px radius, elevation 6. **Menus and popups:** surface, 8px radius, 1px hairline border, elevation 3. **Tooltip:** shell fill (ink in Dark), 8px radius, appears after 500ms.

### Navigation
- **Top bar:** the shell colour, with the 32px Pitchy logo 12px left of the screen name (Title, on-shell) on every screen. It is not centred, has no elevation and is 56px tall. The logo asset is a placeholder until the real one is supplied.
- **Bottom bar (phones):** a surface background with an accent-tint indicator (accent at 14%). The selected item uses ink and a 600 label; unselected items use ink-muted and a 500 label. Outlined icons switch to filled when selected.
- **Rail (wide windows):** an 88px rail on the shell colour with labels always shown. The selected item has a shell-raised indicator with Glacier icon and label; unselected items use on-shell-muted.

### Source Rail (signature)
A card headed "Sources · checked <time> PHT" (Label, ink-muted). Each source is a row with a 24px rail column, where the stops are joined by the rail line, then the short source name (Title small), a dense state pill at the row end and a detail line (Body small). The detail line says "Current until ...", "Fetched ... · 9 h ago", "Updating now", "No forecast stored ..." or the backend's limitation message, and notes when a source doesn't cover the whole match. The time figures are set in Plex Mono digits. Under it, a validity bar draws the forecast window to true time scale across the match coverage window, with kickoff ticked and labelled. The rail only shows what the backend reports; it never judges freshness.

### Weather Value Card
Collapsed, it shows the quantity label, an expand chevron, the value (Headline, ink; ink-muted when not Current) with its unit (Title medium), and the slot time with the source's line dot. A dense state pill appears whenever the state is not Current. Expanding it (220ms, strong ease-out; collapsing 150ms) adds a hairline divider and rows for Source (with line dot), Fetched, Valid, Spread across sources, and any Fallback or Notes.

### Notice Banner and Request States
- **Notice banner:** a full-width strip, 10px x 16px padding, 20px icon or spinner, 12px gap, Body text at 700. Updating uses the Updating colours with a spinner. The saved copy ("Saved copy from <time> PHT, not live") uses the Stale colours.
- **Loading:** skeletons shaped exactly like the content they stand for. **Empty / Error:** centred and at most 420px wide, with a 40px icon, Title medium heading and muted body. Error icons use request-error; "not built yet" uses ink-muted.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour from the palette extensions (PitchyPalette, StateColors, SafetyColors), never from literal colours in widgets.
- **Do** show every data state as a pill with its icon and word, and keep Stale data on screen with its dashed stamp.
- **Do** give each new weather source one fixed, cool line colour (light and dark variants) and use it only for that source's dot, rail stop and validity bar.
- **Do** label every time PHT, and name the day when it isn't today in PHT.
- **Do** colour an official alert only from PAGASA's own level (P-18): watch, yellow rainfall and signal 1 are advisory; advisory, orange rainfall and signals 2-3 are warning; warning, red rainfall and signals 4-5 are danger; anything else stays neutral. Always show PAGASA's words with the colour.
- **Do** use one icon per hazard level (advisory info_outline, warning warning_amber_rounded, danger warning) and colour it from SafetyColors.
- **Do** keep tap targets at least 48px, and keep cards flat with a 1px hairline border and a 12px radius.
- **Do** set Line Board figures (rail times, kickoff time, trend axis and readout) in IBM Plex Mono digits and everything else in Switzer.
- **Do** keep the Pitchy logo left of the screen name in every top bar.
- **Do** make reduced motion opacity-only and no longer than 150ms, and make every exit faster than its entrance.

### Don't:
- **Don't** theme, tint or let users customise the state colours or the safety ramp. Custom changes only the shell and the accent, picked from the eight swatches.
- **Don't** use palette.accent or any themed colour for a hazard level.
- **Don't** use yellow, orange or red as a source line colour, a swatch or decoration.
- **Don't** show any state by colour alone.
- **Don't** add shadows or elevation to cards, bars, chips or the rail; only overlays get the one navy-tinted shadow.
- **Don't** animate tabs, values, safety elements or state elements, and don't use ease-in, bounce, stagger or scale.
- **Don't** show stale, missing or unavailable data in a way that could read as Current, and don't hide it.
- **Don't** set figures without tabular spacing.
- **Don't** load any font at runtime or add a font-fetching package.
- **Don't** bring the landing page's Racing Red, or any landing-page style, into the app.
- **Don't** treat the placeholder logo as final artwork.
