Match conditions, nothing hidden

Pitchy is a weather and safety guide for football matches in the Philippines. It brings several sources together for your venue and kickoff, and keeps the evidence one tap away.

Conditions for your venue and kickoff

Pick Rizal Memorial Stadium or New Clark City, and a kickoff time. Pitchy shows air temperature, heat index, humidity and rain, with more one tap away. Each value names its source and the hour it covers.

  • Open-Meteo
  • PAGASA
  • DOST-ASTI (planned)

Hourly trend around kickoff

Pick a value and a time range, from 6 hours before kickoff to 5 hours after. The chart shows the forecast for each hour and which source it came from. Hours already passed are still forecasts, not measurements, and a missing hour stays a gap. The chart draws no safety limits; those are on the Safety screen.

The safety status, from the rules

Pitchy’s safety engine checks heat, lightning and rain against the approved rules. It shows what the rules say officials should do, and who decides. The decision always stays with the match officials.

Ask Pitchy, in plain words

Ask about the weather and the guidance for your match. Pick your role, such as coach, referee, medical staff or spectator, and answers are worded for you. They’re AI explanations, never official decisions.

App screens with sample data

On Windows, for planning ahead

The same app on a PC, with room to read the sources, the timestamps and the basis of each assessment before match day.

Pitchy tells you how old the data is

A weather number without its age can mislead. Pitchy never shows old or missing data as if it were live.

  • A source on every value

    Each reading names where it came from and the time it covers.

  • Stale is never shown as current

    Old or missing data is labelled Stale or Unavailable, never passed off as live.

  • A saved copy when the signal drops

    Weak signal at the venue? Pitchy shows its last good reply, clearly marked as a saved copy.

  • Philippine Time, always

    Every time is shown in PHT, whatever time zone your device is set to.

Pitchy supports the people who make match decisions. It doesn’t replace them.

Questions

Is Pitchy available yet?

Yes. Version 1.1.0 is out for Android (APK) and Windows (installer). Download it on this page, or from Pitchy’s GitHub Releases, where every version and its SHA-256 checksums are listed.

Is there an iPhone or Mac version?

No. Pitchy is planned for Android and Windows only.

Why does Android ask me to allow the install?

Pitchy for Android comes as an APK file from this page, not from the Google Play Store. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser for files like this.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up or login. In Ask Pitchy you pick a role, which only changes how answers are worded.

Where does the weather come from?

From PAGASA and Open-Meteo, with DOST-ASTI planned. In the app, every value shows its source. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com.

Does Pitchy decide whether a match goes ahead?

No. It shows the data and what the approved rules say. Match officials make the decision.

Is the weather on this page real?

No. The rain and wind on the pitch at the top of this page are just for fun. Real conditions are only in the app.
